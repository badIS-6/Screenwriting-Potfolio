SUBHUMAN / الحيوان

Feature | Psychological drama

An original low-budget script. Never before done in Tunisia. A rarely seen perspective in this genre.
(Language: Tunisian Darija)

Logline

A self-loathing young man tracks the "perfect guy" who stole his girlfriend, leading him into a cult that feeds on male desire for power and love.

Synopsis

Elyes, a physically capable yet self-hating man, becomes obsessed with hunting the white-haired stranger who stole his lover. His search leads him to the Club, a secret fraternity cult that exploits male insecurity through a brutal hierarchy.

At a university, he crosses paths with Rayen, composed yet equally insecure, and leaves him humiliated. After a failed assassination attempt, they form a tense alliance and descend into the Club's violence, while Rayen's girlfriend Yakine is drawn into danger.

As Elyes nears his target, he begins to embody the very logic he despises, forcing Rayen to question him. At the center, Elyes is offered power in exchange for killing Rayen. He refuses, exposing the emptiness of what he pursued.

In the aftermath, Rayen saves Yakine and restores himself. Elyes is left hollow, facing a void that pushes him to the brink.
