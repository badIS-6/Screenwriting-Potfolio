Inspirations 🔍

I've been practicing storytelling since I was 6 years old, stories, books ... Many of my writings did not see the light of day but my love for movies made me realize that screenwriting was my thing.

My work explores how systems shape identity, focuses on individuals defined by hierarchy, status, and the need for validation. Rather than portraying exceptional characters, I study how ordinary people internalize the structures of power, often pursuing goals that ultimately undermine them.

My approach is grounded and restrained, relying on behavior, silence, and subtext over exposition. My stories rely on psychological tension and contradictions. Visually and structurally, the work emphasizes clarity and flexibility.

At its core, my work is concerned with how people navigate what they cannot escape (class, looks, addiction...), and what remains of them when those systems are fully confronted.
